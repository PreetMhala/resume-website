Developing a Custom Diffusion Noise Schedule (EDM2, CelebA)

Stony Brook University • Prof. Dimitris Samaras

Oct 2025 – Dec 2025 Generative AI

Investigated how different noise schedules affect training stability, convergence speed, and sample quality in modern diffusion models using NVIDIA’s EDM2-XS architecture.

Results

  • Tested 5 noise schedules: Karras-ρ, Linear, Cosine, Logarithmic,, and Quadratic.
  • Best FID of 10.75 on CelebA test set using Linear, Logarithmic, and Quadratic schedules.
  • Conditional diffusion achieved attribute-conditioned FID ≈ 20.26.
